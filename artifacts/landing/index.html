The Process

How TraffIQ Works

Three simple steps turn millions of daily journeys into actionable road intelligence.

1

Download & Join

Road users download the TraffIQ mobile app. Choose your transport mode — car, boda boda, bus, or walking — and start your journey. GPS tracking begins automatically.

2

Every Trip Feeds the Grid

Speed, location, acceleration, and braking data flow from every active user into the live traffic engine. Road segments update every 5 seconds with crowd-verified conditions.

3

Smarter, Safer Roads

Authorities monitor the live dashboard. Drivers get safety scores. Commuters receive real-time alerts. Cities get the data they need to reduce accidents and congestion.
